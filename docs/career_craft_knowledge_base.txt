# CareerCraft Project Knowledge Base
# Comprehensive documentation of all platform features for AI training.

## 1. Project Identity & Mission
CareerCraft is a premium career development platform that leverages AI to provide a high-end, interactive preparation environment for job seekers. It combines AI-driven coaching (interviews and resumes) with a social learning community and a comprehensive course-based gamification system.

## 2. Technology Stack
- Framework: Next.js (App Router)
- Styling: Vanilla CSS (Custom premium aesthetics: glassmorphism, smooth animations)
- Backend: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, Cloud Functions, Storage)
- AI Integration: 
    - Google Gemini: For interview feedback, resume analysis, and coding feedback.
    - VAPI: Real-time AI voice interaction for mock interviews.
- Payment Gateway: PayHere integration for course purchases.
- Icons: Lucide React.
- Typography: Modern fonts (Geist, Outfit, Inter).

## 3. User Roles
- **Student (User)**: Can participate in mock interviews, upload resumes for analysis, enroll in courses, earn XP/badges, and interact socially.
- **Tutor**: Verified users who can create, manage, and sell courses. They have access to a specialized Tutor Dashboard with earnings and student analytics.
- **Admin**: Platform managers who oversee tutor applications, moderate courses, and manage reported content.

## 4. AI Tutor & Voice Interview
- **AI Personal Tutor**: The platform provides a personalized AI Tutor experience during mock interviews.
- **Voice Interview**: 
    - Real-time conversation powered by VAPI.
    - Users can interact with an AI interviewer using their voice.
    - Features include mute/unmute controls and "End Call" functionality.
- **Resume Personalization**:
    - Users can upload a **Resume (PDF)** during interview setup.
    - The AI Tutor analyzes the resume's skills, projects, and experience to tailor questions specifically to the candidate.
    - The interviewer is briefed with a candidate summary and name extracted from the resume for a highly realistic experience.

## 5. Live Coding Interview & Compiler
- **Coding Mode**: A specialized interview mode for technical roles.
- **Interactive Code Editor**: A full-featured IDE integrated into the interview session.
- **Built-in Compiler/Execution Engine**:
    - Users can write and run code in real-time.
    - Supports multiple programming languages (e.g., JavaScript, Python).
    - AI provides immediate feedback on code logic, complexity, and best practices.
- **Practical Challenges**: In coding mode, the AI Tutor focuses on "Write a function..." style practical tasks rather than just theory.

## 6. AI Resume Analysis
- **Resume Scan**: Users upload PDF or DOCX files.
- **ATS Compatibility**: The AI analyzes the resume against industry standards and specific job descriptions.
- **Optimization Suggestions**: Provides actionable feedback on formatting, keyword optimization, and content improvements to pass Applicant Tracking Systems.

## 7. AI Job Recommendations
- **Automated Matching**: The system extracts skills and experience from the user's latest resume using AI.
- **Real-time Job Search**: Generates optimized search queries and fetches live job listings from external APIs like FindWork.dev and Jobicy.
- **AI Ranking**: A career coach AI ranks the jobs based on match percentage (0-100%) and provides a specific reason why each job is a good fit.

## 8. Course Builder & LMS
- **Course Builder (Tutors Only)**:
    - Dedicated interface for tutors to create and structure courses.
    - Ability to add **Chapters** and **Lessons**.
    - Supports both **Text-based** and **Video-based** lessons.
    - Tutors can set a **Price** (in LKR) and upload a **Cover Image**.
- **Course Enrollment**: Students can browse the community, purchase courses via PayHere, and track progress.
- **Reporting**: 
    - Users can "Report" (flag) a course for inappropriate content or low quality.
    - Reports are sent to the Admin Dashboard for moderation.

## 9. Blog & Resources
- **Industry Insights**: Professional articles on interview tips, career growth, and industry trends.
- **Interactive Content**: Engaging posts to help users stay informed about the evolving job market.

## 10. Gamification System
- **Career XP**: Users earn 10 XP for every lesson completed.
- **Tiers**: Progress through levels based on total XP:
    - Novice: 0 - 99 XP
    - Explorer: 100 - 499 XP
    - Scholar: 500 - 999 XP
    - Master: 1000+ XP
- **Badges**: Awarded automatically upon completion of an entire course. Badges are visually premium and shareable on LinkedIn.

## 11. Social Networking Features
- **Profiles**: Public-facing user profiles showing their tier, XP, badges, and shared activity.
- **Friends System**:
    - Search for other users and send friend requests.
    - Suggestion Engine: Recommends friends based on mutual connections, same tier, and similar XP.
- **Messaging**: Real-time direct messaging between friends with integrated notifications.
- **Community Sharing**: Users can publish their mock interview results or success stories to the community feed.

## 12. Dashboards & Financing
- **Student Dashboard**: Overview of enrolled courses, recent interview results, friends list, and current gamification status (XP/Tier).
- **Tutor Dashboard & Financing**:
    - **Revenue Tracking**: View total estimated revenue from course sales (in LKR).
    - **Earnings Breakdown**: Detailed analytics showing revenue from "paid" enrollments.
    - **Student Analytics**: Total student count (paid, free, and completed enrollments).
    - **Course Performance**: Analytics chart showing top courses by views.
    - **Banking**: Manage bank account details for payouts and earnings withdrawal.
- **Admin Dashboard**:
    - **Tutor Verification**: Review CVs and details of tutor applicants. Approve or Reject status.
    - **Content Moderation**: Review reported courses and take action (dismiss or delete).

## 13. Site Navigation & User Flow
To provide step-by-step guidance, the chatbot must be familiar with the following paths:

### Public Pages (Accessible to all)
- **Home**: `/` - Landing page with platform overview.
- **Blog**: `/blog` - Articles and career resources.
- **Community**: `/community` - Public feed of shared interviews and courses.
- **Resume Analysis**: `/resume` - Direct access to AI resume tools.
- **Privacy Policy**: `/privacy` - Data protection and AI processing details.
- **Terms & Conditions**: `/terms` - Platform usage rules and legal agreements.
- **Login/Register**: `/login`, `/register`.

### Student (User) Navigation
Accessed via the **User Sidebar**:
1. **Overview**: `/user` - Main student dashboard with XP/Tier stats.
2. **Interviews**: `/user/interviews` - History of mock interviews and AI feedback reports.
3. **Resume**: `/user/resume` - Management of uploaded resumes and analysis history.
4. **Community**: `/user/community` - Browse and learn from other users' shared interviews.
5. **Friends**: `/user/friends` - Social hub to search for peers, manage requests, and chat.
6. **Settings**: `/user/settings` - Profile and account preferences.

### Tutor Navigation
Accessed via the **Tutor Sidebar**:
1. **Overview**: `/tutor` - Tutor-specific dashboard with course and student analytics.
2. **Courses**: `/tutor/courses` - Access to the **Course Builder** to manage chapters and lessons.
3. **Interviews**: `/tutor/interviews` - Tutor's personal interview history.
4. **Earnings**: `/tutor/earnings` - Financial overview, revenue tracking, and payout management.
5. **Friends/Community**: Similar to student roles but within the tutor workspace.
6. **Settings**: `/tutor/settings` - Professional profile and banking details.

### Admin Navigation
Accessed via the **Admin Sidebar**:
1. **Overview**: `/admin` - System-wide stats.
2. **Users**: `/admin/users` - User management and **Tutor Verification** (Review CVs).
3. **Payouts**: `/admin/payouts` - Managing and approving tutor earnings withdrawals.
4. **Courses & Reports**: `/admin/courses` - **Content Moderation** for reported or flagged courses.

## 14. Common Workflows & Troubleshooting
- **Starting an Interview**: Navigate to **Interviews** -> Click **"New Interview"** -> Configure settings (Role, Topic, Mode) -> Start.
- **Creating a Course**: Go to **Tutor Dashboard** -> **Courses** -> Click **"Create Course"** -> Use the **Course Builder** to add content.
- **Checking Earnings**: Tutors should navigate to the **Earnings** tab to see their total revenue in LKR and payout status.
- **Finding Friends**: Go to the **Friends** tab -> Use the **Search Bar** or check **Friend Suggestions**.
